Our Story

A Welcoming Community of Faith

Eastminster Presbyterian Church is a PCUSA congregation rooted in the Hyattsville community, serving Prince George's County, Maryland. We are a church family committed to worship, discipleship, and serving our neighbors with the love of Christ.
